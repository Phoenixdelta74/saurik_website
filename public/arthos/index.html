Where your records live

On your computer

Invoices, customers, products, payments, and expenses are stored in the browser on the computer where you use Arthos. You are responsible for exporting backups and storing copies safely. A backup does not restore activity created after it was made, and clearing the browser's site data removes the records on that computer.

With your Arthos account

Your sign-in email, business name, optional GSTIN, and licence status are stored with SAURIK IT so you can sign in and keep your licence. Your invoices and business records are not uploaded.

Backup files contain your business records but not passwords or sign-in details. When you export a file or send a document by email, the information you select is shared through that service.

Common questions

Which edition of Arthos is available?

At launch, Arthos runs in current Chrome or Edge on Windows, with your records kept in that browser on your computer. A separate Arthos Cloud edition, with records stored on our servers and access from several devices, is planned but not available yet.

Does Arthos need an internet connection?

You need internet to sign in with the code sent to your email and to refresh your licence from time to time. Once signed in, Arthos keeps working offline until your licence end date. Sending invoices through Gmail also needs internet.

Can I use Arthos on another computer?

Yes. Sign in on that computer and restore a backup there. Records do not synchronise between computers, so each computer keeps its own copy from the point of restore.

Where are my records and backups stored?

Invoices and business records are stored in the browser on your computer, and you are responsible for exporting backups. Your sign-in email, business name, optional GSTIN, and licence status are stored with SAURIK IT. Backup files do not include passwords or sign-in details.

What happens when the 30-day trial ends?

Arthos becomes read-only. You can still open every screen, print, export, and restore a backup, so your records stay available. Creating or editing invoices and other records needs a paid licence. Pricing will be published before launch. In the meantime, contact us for a live demo or to discuss current pricing and availability.

Does Arthos file GST returns?

Arthos supports GST-aware documents and selected accounting exports. It does not directly file GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, or other GST returns unless that capability is explicitly introduced and verified in the product.

Will I be able to move to Arthos Cloud later?

Arthos Cloud is planned but not available yet. Any move to it will be a migration from a backup that you review and confirm. Automatic migration or synchronisation is not currently offered.
