# Arthos Induction into the SAURIK IT Growth and Positioning Blueprint

**Status:** Strategic proposal for review  
**Related sources:** `SAURIK_IT_CUSTOMER_GROWTH_AND_POSITIONING_BLUEPRINT.md` and `ARTHOS-LANDING-PAGE-SPEC-v2.md`  
**Recommendation:** Incorporate into a future blueprint v1.1 after founder review

## Executive recommendation

Do not introduce Arthos as another equal headline service. That would weaken the
blueprint's central argument that SAURIK IT must avoid appearing broad and
unfocused.

Introduce Arthos as a second, clearly differentiated product motion under one
coherent operational-software strategy:

- **Saurik Track** controls work outside the office: field activity, visits,
  vehicles, and mobile stock.
- **Arthos** controls the commercial workflow around billing: invoices,
  collections, pricing history, and business visibility.
- **Custom software** handles workflows that neither packaged product fits.
- **Hardware** remains a regional supporting capability.

This creates a credible field-activity-to-invoice-to-collection portfolio story
without claiming that Track and Arthos integrate.

## 1. Revise the executive hierarchy

Replace the existing three-layer hierarchy with:

1. **Primary market wedge:** Field distribution and mobile operations, led by
   Saurik Track.
2. **Emerging adjacent product:** Invoicing, collections, and business visibility
   for small Indian businesses, led by Arthos.
3. **Secondary service:** Custom operational software, data analytics, and
   carefully bounded AI workflow automation.
4. **Regional supporting service:** CCTV, computers, and server installation or
   service in confirmed serviceable areas.

Add this strategic boundary:

> Arthos is an adjacent product-validation motion, not permission to reposition
> SAURIK IT as a generic ERP or accounting-software company. Saurik Track remains
> the primary acquisition wedge until customer evidence supports changing that
> priority.

## 2. Add Arthos product positioning

Add a dedicated subsection after the Saurik Track product positioning:

> **Arthos Invoice Studio helps small Indian service and trading businesses
> create GST-aware business documents, track collections, preserve pricing
> history, and understand performance from the records they enter. Arthos
> Desktop supports offline operation and locally controlled records on a Windows
> computer; Arthos Cloud is intended for authenticated remote access with
> cloud-stored records and backups.**

Supporting rules:

- Arthos is not complete statutory accounting software.
- Arthos does not claim direct GST-return filing.
- Profitability figures must remain qualified as estimated.
- Desktop and Cloud are separate editions with different storage and access
  models.
- Do not imply automatic synchronization or migration between editions.
- Public conversion messaging remains pre-launch until the complete trial and
  paid-access journeys are verified.

## 3. Add a bounded Arthos validation lane

Do not make Arthos an unbounded fourth sales experiment. Add a smaller
product-demand validation lane.

### Problem hypothesis

Small service and trading businesses may struggle with repeated invoice entry,
partial-payment tracking, overdue follow-up, historical pricing, and repeatedly
rebuilt spreadsheet reports.

### Discovery questions

1. Which business documents are prepared repeatedly?
2. How are partial payments and overdue balances tracked?
3. How frequently do item prices change?
4. Which reports are rebuilt manually?
5. Why would the business prefer Desktop, Cloud, or remain undecided?
6. Does the business need invoicing support or complete accounting and GST
   filing?
7. What access and export behaviour would it expect after trial expiry?

### Evidence required

- Qualified early-access requests
- Demonstration attendance
- Desktop, Cloud, or undecided edition preference
- Repeated problem patterns from discovery
- Willingness to start a verified trial
- Willingness to pay after the trial

Add this product-routing rule:

> Recommend Track for field execution and mobile-stock problems, Arthos for
> invoicing and collection problems, and custom development when the required
> workflow does not fit either packaged product.

## 4. Add a dedicated Arthos ICP

### ICP: Small Indian service or trading business

**Buyer:** Owner, finance or administration lead, or billing operator  
**Current behaviour:** Word or Excel invoices, disconnected payment notes,
manual overdue follow-up, changing price lists, and spreadsheet reporting  
**Trigger:** Increasing invoice workload, collection delays, pricing disputes,
lost history, need for remote access, or preference for offline control  
**Product fit:** Arthos Desktop, Arthos Cloud, or an early-access conversation
while the edition choice remains unclear  
**Disqualifier:** Requires complete statutory accounts, direct GST filing,
enterprise ERP, verified Desktop-to-Cloud synchronization, or unsupported data
migration

Do not invent employee-count or revenue thresholds until discovery provides
evidence.

## 5. Add an Arthos offer and conversion path

Use this staged path:

1. Request early access.
2. Qualify the invoicing, collection, pricing, reporting, and edition need.
3. Offer a product demonstration when a representative workflow exists.
4. Invite the prospect to the 60-day trial only after the relevant edition's
   complete journey is verified.
5. Offer paid access using owner-approved pricing, licence scope, billing,
   expiry, support, terms, and privacy rules.

Do not treat an early-access request, CTA click, email draft, or WhatsApp handoff
as a trial start or received enquiry.

## 7. Update lead capture and CRM

The main enquiry flow should offer context-specific choices rather than one
undifferentiated product enquiry:

1. Request a workflow review
2. Request a Saurik Track pilot
3. Request Arthos early access
4. Discuss a custom project

Add CRM fields for:

- Product interest: Track, Arthos, custom software, AI or data, hardware
- Arthos edition interest: Desktop, Cloud, undecided
- Current invoicing method
- Current collection-tracking method
- Trial status: unavailable, eligible, invited, activated, completed
- Commercial status
- Source page and campaign

Keep these definitions separate:

- A CTA click is not a lead.
- An email or WhatsApp handoff is not a received enquiry.
- An early-access request is not a trial start.
- Trial activation is not a paying customer.

## 8. Add an Arthos content cluster

### Core topic cluster: Invoicing, collections, and business visibility

- Desktop invoicing versus cloud invoicing for small businesses
- Tracking partial payments without rebuilding spreadsheets
- Why historical invoices should preserve their original prices
- GST-aware invoicing versus GST-return filing
- What estimated profit means in business software
- Local backups and the customer's responsibilities
- Questions to answer before moving invoicing data to the cloud
- Choosing invoicing software without buying a full ERP

Content should educate around genuine buying decisions rather than compete on
generic claims such as "best billing software."

## 9. Add product-specific success metrics

Track Arthos separately from Track pilots and custom-project leads:

- Qualified visits to `/arthos/`
- Edition-selection engagement
- Early-access CTA starts
- Completed, confirmed contact submissions or handoffs
- Qualified Arthos discovery conversations
- Desktop versus Cloud preference
- Product demonstrations
- Trial invitations and activations after launch
- Trial-to-paid conversion after paid access exists
- Disqualification reasons
- Onboarding and support burden

Do not combine Track pilots, Arthos early access, and custom-project enquiries
into one product-conversion metric.

## 10. Add risks and mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio dilution | Two products make SAURIK IT harder to understand | Retain Track as the primary wedge and present Arthos as an adjacent product |
| Edition confusion | Visitors assume Desktop includes or synchronizes with Cloud | Repeat edition-specific storage, backup, connectivity, and access boundaries |
| Category confusion | Prospects assume Arthos is full accounting or GST-filing software | Lead with invoicing and collections and state limitations explicitly |
| Premature conversion | Marketing sends visitors to an unfinished installer, signup, trial, or checkout | Keep the centrally controlled pre-launch state until the full journey passes verification |
| Support fragmentation | Desktop and Cloud create different onboarding and recovery obligations | Define separate support, backup, activation, authentication, and recovery procedures |
| SEO cannibalization | Generic invoicing content enters a crowded category without differentiation | Publish problem-led content for the defined ICP and avoid unsupported superlatives |

## 11. Add founder decisions

Add the following to the founder-decision register:

- Final Arthos production URL
- Desktop and Cloud readiness status
- Desktop licence price, duration, activation, and offline trial enforcement
- Cloud price, billing period, renewal, cancellation, tenant isolation, and
  authentication readiness
- Whether payment details are required for the trial
- Access, export, and record handling after trial expiry for each edition
- Support channels, hours, and response commitments
- Applicable privacy, terms, refund, and data-processing documents
- Desktop-to-Cloud migration availability
- Which capabilities are verified in each edition

## 12. Recommended operating principle

Arthos strengthens the growth blueprint when it demonstrates product discipline,
not when it simply increases the number of things SAURIK IT appears to sell.

Recommended portfolio statement:

> **SAURIK IT builds practical operational products for the moments where
> business records become unclear: Track for field execution and mobile stock,
> Arthos for invoicing and collections, and carefully scoped custom systems for
> the gaps between them.**

The governing rule should remain: earn expansion through customer evidence,
product readiness, truthful claims, and separately measured acquisition motions.
